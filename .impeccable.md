## Design Context

### Users
CineTrekker serves cinephiles and casual movie or TV viewers who want to discover titles, keep a personal watchlist, and remember what they have watched. Guest use should remain useful, with independent accounts available for syncing across devices.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, calm, dependable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing film-focused identity and its light, dark, and OLED themes. Favor clear editorial hierarchy, restrained surfaces, and predictable feedback over decorative effects.

### Design Principles
- Make title discovery and personal tracking feel reliable, even when provider data is delayed or unavailable.
- Keep loading, empty, and error feedback concise and actionable.
- Preserve the current consumer-app workflows and independent-account model.
- Adapt controls and content spacing for touch without hiding core functionality.

# Profile benchmark research notes

## Verified findings

- **Letterboxd:** Profiles separate core account areas (Profile, Films, Diary, Watchlist), allow a short profile identity layer (name, location, website, bio, favourite films), and turn dated logs into a Diary and Profile Recent Activity. Its engagement loop combines follows, a personalized activity stream, likes/reviews, public lists, and annual/all-time stats. Its published stats include weekly/yearly patterns, decades, most watched/highest rated actors and directors, milestone-list progress, and genre/language/country breakdowns. Source: https://letterboxd.com/welcome/ and https://letterboxd.com/about/.
- **IMDb:** A user profile aggregates ratings, reviews, lists, Watchlist, Check-ins, polls, and earned badges. It exposes privacy controls by content type and Ratings Insights that show distributions over rating values, interests, and release years, including comparison with other public users. Source: https://help.imdb.com/article/imdb/general-information/faq-for-the-your-profile-feature/GJSRJTR7G24USEPC.

## CineTrekker profile inventory from source and supplied screenshot

The current profile presents: identity header with avatar, level/progress and three headline metrics; overview/favorites/taste/edit tabs; an awards/achievement callout; cinephile rank; recently watched shelf; actor-match cards; separate favourite movie and series shelves; genre pills; rating and decade distributions; and crew insights. It also offers avatar/bio/favourite-genre/public-profile configuration, profile sharing, pinned favourites, and carousel controls. The supplied screenshot shows a long, dashboard-like single page with dense lower-page analytics.
- **Trakt:** Recent product updates describe viewing streaks, Profile Activity, Screen Time (last seven days and peak hours), All Time Stats, monthly and annual recaps, follower activity/follow requests, ratings and reviews in social activity, public/private lists, and native user profiles. The update also highlights the risk of over-prioritizing large imagery and hiding core tracking state behind extra clicks; community feedback calls for immediate watched/list feedback and information density. Source: https://forums.trakt.tv/t/trakt-product-update-june-mid-july-2026/116109.
- **TV Time:** The now-historical product positioned personal viewing stats, recommendations, ratings, spoiler-safe community reactions, custom shareable lists, custom posters, and badges as core experiences. Its annual Rewind gave users a shareable year-end recap of favorite shows, movies, characters, genres, and every tracked episode/movie. Sources: https://apps.apple.com/iq/app/tv-time-track-shows-movies/id431065232 and https://whipmedia.com/news/tv-time-unveils-tv-time-rewind-2022/.
- **SIMKL:** Emphasizes control and personalization: public/private/unlisted profile and list visibility, custom lists and collections, favorite-list privacy/background customization, sorting/filtering, quick edits, and visible Pro badges. Source: https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/account-and-billing/subscription-plans/pro.
- **Serializd:** Turns status into an actionable profile model: Currently Watching, Watched, Watchlist, Paused, and Dropped each influence profile placement, alerts, home modules, and recommendations. Dated logs create Recent Activity and a Diary; logs with review text populate a Reviews tab and followers’ feeds. Its product positioning reinforces tracking, ratings/reviews, diary, following, discussions, member lists, and recommendations. Sources: https://serializd.com/about?q=tracking and https://www.serializd.com/.
- **MyAnimeList:** The inspected public profile prioritizes scannable account state: joined/last online identity, lists, statistics, history, favorites, visible counts for community contribution types, badges, friends, granular status counts (watching/completed/on-hold/dropped/plan to watch), days watched, mean score, episode totals, a latest-update history, favorites, and comments. It is feature-rich but visually dated, text-heavy, and exposes too many low-signal counters at once. Source: https://myanimelist.net/profile/anime-prime.

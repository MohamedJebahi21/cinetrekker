## Design Context

### Users
- **Audience**: Both casual and power users across devices (desktop, mobile)
- **Use Case**: Movie and TV show tracking, discovery, watchlist management, and following updates
- **Context**: Used both at home (desktop/laptop) and on-the-go (mobile)

### Brand Personality
- **Personality**: Cinematic & Premium
- **Emotional Goal**: Evoke a movie theater-like experience while being functional and trustworthy
- **Tone**: Elegant, film-focused, high-quality feel

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Reference**: Letterboxd (community-focused film tracking with clean design)
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode (user choice) - dark mode should feel cinematic, light mode clean and bright
- **Key Differentiator**: Focus on cinematic presentation while remaining highly functional

### Design Principles
1. **Cinematic First**: Visuals should evoke the feeling of watching movies in a theater
2. **Functional & Intuitive**: Prioritize usability for both casual and power users
3. **Premium Feel**: Use high-quality UI elements, animations, and spacing
4. **Responsive & Accessible**: Work flawlessly across all device sizes and be accessible to all users
